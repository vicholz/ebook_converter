    Private by design

    Conversion runs in WebAssembly in this tab. Books are not sent to a server.

    DRM will fail

    Amazon-encrypted Kindle files, Adobe DRM EPUBs, and password-protected comics cannot be converted. DRM-free AZW3, EPUB, MOBI, CBZ, and CBR can.

    Where the files go

    Copy .azw3 or .mobi into a Kindle documents folder. .epub opens in most other reading apps. MOBI keeps the cover, chapters, and pictures; it does not keep fixed layout or embedded fonts.
